## Reading

- **t_hi follows the bright tail, not the overall brightness.** Across the 8 images t_hi runs from
  0.523 (682_z29) to 0.647 (542_z06). It rises with the saturated fraction (rho 0.98) and with the
  99th percentile (rho 0.73), and is unrelated to the mean (0.21) or median (-0.05). It sits at 0.63
  to 0.75 of the 99th percentile and leaves 2.1 to 5.2% of the pixels above it. So it moves with how
  much bright material the field holds (the lacunae and canals themselves).
- **t_lo is a fixed share of the image's own contrast.** It is 0.183 to 0.196 of the preprocessed
  99th percentile in every image (rho 0.99) and leaves 18 to 24% of the preprocessed pixels above it.
  Because the preprocessed channel is divided by its own maximum, the same cut in raw units varies
  2.7 fold, from 0.027 (543 images) to 0.071 (542_z06), following the brightest top-hat response
  (rho 0.90).
- **Both cuts are image relative by design**, so a dim field and a bright field are cut at the same
  rank, not at the same intensity. That keeps the share of network pixels similar between images,
  but it means a between-image difference in true network amount is partly absorbed by the cut.
- **The visibly missed bodies of 2.1 are not lost at the cut.** All three are above t_hi: two are
  removed by a shape filter (aspect 6.03 against 6.0, solidity 0.452 against 0.5) and one by area
  (301 px^2, cut by the frame edge).
