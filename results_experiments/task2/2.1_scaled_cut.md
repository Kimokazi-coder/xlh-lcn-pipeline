## Reading

- 543_3 (40,310) is kept at 0.8 and 0.9 times t_hi (the lower cut adds width, so the aspect falls to
  5.43 and 4.15), and lost at 1.0 and above.
- 542_z06 (230,300) never passes as one object. At 0.9 its lower part is kept alone (684 px^2); at 1.1
  and 1.2 it splits into two kept lacunae. By eye it is one body, so a higher cut would count it twice.
- 542_z06 (5,930) stays under 400 px^2 at every scale; it is cut by the frame edge.
- So no single scale of t_hi recovers the three bodies, and the scales that recover one change the
  others. The losses come from the filters acting on the shapes the cut produces.
