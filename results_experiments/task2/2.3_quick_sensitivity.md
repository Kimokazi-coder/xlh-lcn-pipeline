- **Bridges** move irregularly (542_z06: 17 to 14 and 8; 682_z29: 17 to 17 and 29), as expected for a

- The direction is the same in every image: a lower cut gives bigger lacunae, more roots, more ring
  length and more skeleton. The size of the move differs between images (682_z29 roots +31% at 0.9,
  543-2 +6.6%), so the cuts do not shift all images alike.
