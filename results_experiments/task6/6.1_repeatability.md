## Reading

- **The 8 images are sections of 3 or 4 fields.** At zero shift, 5 pairs share 77 to 92% of their
  lacunae (chance level 0.2 to 0.4 matches), and 21 pairs share none. The largest gap in the sorted
  match fractions (0.40 to 0.77) gives 4 groups: 542_z06 + 542_z18; 543-2 + 543_3 + 543_z13;
  682_z23 + 682_z29; and 682_z08 alone. Matched centroids sit a median 5 to 13 px apart, so the
  fields are hardly shifted between sections.
- **682_z08 probably belongs to the 682 field** but at a depth where most cells differ: it shares 4 of
  its 10 lacunae with 682_z23 at zero shift (15 times the chance level of 0.26) and 1 with 682_z29.
  A translation search finds at most 6 matches, but the same search also finds 3 between unrelated
  fields, so it does not settle the question. It is kept as its own group, the conservative reading,
  and listed under Decisions needed. With it, the data agree with Karim's 3 fields.
- **Field density is the most repeatable measure**: within a group the images differ by 1.7 to 3.0%
  (range over mean). Ring 30 px per cell and ring 60 px per cell come next at the image level (1.2 to
  3.5% in the 542 and 543 groups, 12 to 13% in the 682 group), then roots per cell (4 to 19%).
- **Per matched cell, every per-cell measure changes a lot between sections.** Median relative
  difference over 43 interior pairs: roots 0.40, ring 30 px 0.25, ring 60 px 0.14, owned length 0.86,
  edge count 0.86. The values of the same cell in two sections barely correlate (Spearman 0.06 for
  roots, 0.21 for ring 30, 0.12 for ring 60, negative for owned length and edge count).
- **The ownership measures are the least stable** at both levels (owned length and edge count up to
  23 to 29% between images of one field, 86% per cell), which supports keeping them out of the headline.
- **Adjacent sections also differ in true content.** The matched cell's own area differs by a median
  40% between sections (Spearman 0.25): each section cuts the cell at a different depth, so different
  roots and threads lie in the plane. A per-cell difference here is not all measurement error. Lacuna
  counts also differ within a field (16 against 12 in 542), because cells enter and leave the plane.
- **Consequence for the analysis.** Sections of one field are not independent samples: per-cell values
  pooled over images count the same cells more than once (task 4.1), and a per-field mean over its
  sections is the natural unit. Which unit the thesis uses is under Decisions needed.
