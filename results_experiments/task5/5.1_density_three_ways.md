## Reading

- **The denominator choice moves field density by at most a few percent.**
  - Removing the flagged canal mask from skeleton and area: -1.6% (682_z29) to +1.1% (682_z08); 0 in
    the 543 images, which have no flagged structure. It removes 3 to 7% of the field area.
  - Restricting to the draft bone ROI: 0 (543) to +3.1% (682_z08). It removes 0 to 4.2% of the area,
    mostly no-tissue corners and purple edges, which hold little skeleton (density 0 to 0.023 against
    0.034 to 0.045 for the field), so density rises.
  - Both together: -0.6% to +4.5%, largest in 682_z08.
- **The canal regions are not empty and not denser.** Skeleton density inside the flagged mask is
  0.030 to 0.045, against 0.034 to 0.038 for the same fields: removing them barely changes density,
  as round 1 found.
- **The between-image ordering does not change.** The 543 images stay highest (0.044 to 0.045) and the
  542 and 682 images stay at 0.034 to 0.038 under every denominator, so the main between-field
  difference is not a denominator effect.
- The ROI is a draft; with hand-edited masks in `roi_edited/` this table is recomputed from them
  (the roi_source column says which was used).
