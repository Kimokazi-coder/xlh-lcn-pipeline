## Reading

**What the channels hold.**
- **Green is empty in all 8 images**: mean 0.7 to 1.0, maximum 13 to 18 of 255. No structure.
- **Blue carries four things:**
  1. A weak copy of the red structures in every image. Kept lacunae are 1.5 to 2.3 times the matrix
     blue (e.g. 542_z06 42.8 against 18.7), skeleton pixels slightly above the matrix, and blue
     correlates with red at 0.34 to 0.49 per pixel. This looks like bleed-through of the red label or a
     weak second label, not an independent structure.
  2. The canal regions: inside the flagged mask blue is 1.4 to 2.0 times the matrix (542_z06 31.5,
     682_z08 41.7). The red is also bright there, so the ratio stays low and the purple rule does not
     fire inside the flagged mask (overlap 0 in every image).
  3. Purple regions, where blue is high and red is low: the lower right of 542_z06 (a meshy region
     with blue up to 253, also visible as purple in the RGB), the bottom and right edges of 682_z08,
     the lower right of 542_z18, a trace in 682_z23. None in 682_z29 or the 543 images. In 542_z06 a
     few small purple specks also fire in dim matrix; they are part of the draft and may need removing.
  4. No-tissue corners in the 682 images: smoothed blue 3 to 6 against 12 to 21 in the matrix, about
     0.9% of each field (the dark lower left corner of 682_z08, and the strips below the curved bottom
     edge of 682_z23 and 682_z29).

**The draft ROI** removes 0% (543 images), 1.2 to 2.9% (542 images) and 0.9 to 4.1% (682 images) of
the field. No kept lacuna lies outside it. The top band of 682_z08 is bright in red, so it is in the
ROI; it is flagged as a canal structure instead. The vertical band of 542_z06 below the flagged part
(y > 650) is only partly purple (5.3).

**What is not settled.** The no-tissue threshold sits in a dip shared by the three 682 images. The
purple threshold does not sit in any gap; it is anchored on the largest ratio in the three 543 images,
which have no purple region by eye. Both are drafts for Karim to correct, for example by editing the
masks: a hand-edited mask placed in `roi_edited/<image>.png` (white = bone) replaces the draft in every
experiment that uses the ROI (5.1, 5.4).
