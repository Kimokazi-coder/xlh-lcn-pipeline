# 5.4 ROI overlays for all 8 images

Pre-validation. `5.4_roi_overlays/<image>_roi_overlay.png`: red and blue channels (magenta),
no tissue grey, purple cyan, flagged canal mask yellow, kept lacunae green, ROI border white, at
half size. The title line says whether the draft or a hand-edited mask was used.

Hand-edited masks: see `roi_edited/README.txt`. The script reads `roi_edited/<image>.png` (white =
bone) in place of the draft. Self-test (`5.4_edited_mask_selftest.json`): a test mask for 543-2
with x < 512 removed was read as 'edited', correctly (True); density in the right half
0.04683 against 0.04470 for the whole field.
## Reading

- The draft ROI keeps 95.9% (682_z08) to 100% (543 images) of each field.
- What the draft catches: the no-tissue corners of the 682 images and the strongly purple regions of
  542_z06, 542_z18 and 682_z08.
- What it misses: the weaker purple patches at the lower right of 682_z23 and 682_z29, visible in the
  RGB, have a smoothed blue over red of 0.6 to 0.77, mostly under the 0.72 anchor, so they stay in the
  ROI. The flagged top band of 682_z08 and 682_z23 stays in the ROI because its red is bright.
- So the draft is a starting point for hand editing, not a final mask. The edited-mask path is in
  place and tested.
