## Reading

- The line is traced as one continuous thread over 310 of the 311 rows, about 380 px of skeleton, 1.1% of
  the image's skeleton. Removing it would lower field density by 1.1%.
- The flagged canal mask covers only its top 60 rows, so hysteresis and bridging are blocked there
  but not below. The draft ROI (5.2) covers 29 px of it. Neither rule removes it.
- The component it belongs to is much larger (1902 px, 5.4% of the skeleton): horizontal threads join
  it, so through ownership it links cells on both sides of the band. Ring lengths count it only where
  it passes within 30 or 60 px of a lacuna.
- In the raw crop the line is a thin, bright, straight vertical structure, unlike the wavy canaliculi
  around it. It is most likely the wall of the vascular band, not a canaliculus; Karim to confirm.
