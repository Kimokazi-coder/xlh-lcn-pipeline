## Reading

- **None of the three named measures separates the band objects on its own.**
  - Minor axis: 11.5 and 19.8 px. 682_z29 lacuna 3 at (449,250) sits at 12.3 px, and 7 other
    lacunae lie below 19.8 px.
  - Flagged overlap: both are fully inside the flagged mask (1.000), but so are 6 other kept
    lacunae, including ordinary ones such as 542_z06 (573,482) and 542_z18 (639,130). The overlap is
    0 or 1 for every kept lacuna, so it only says "lies in a canal region".
  - Solidity: 0.767 and 0.677, inside the bulk (16 kept lacunae are lower than 0.767).
- **The measure that separates them is the share of area left after an opening with the top-hat disk
  (r = 5 px)**: 0.207 and 0.348, against 0.681 for the next kept lacuna (542_z18 (914,16)). The gap is
  0.333, half the next value. This is the round 3 finding, unchanged.
- **Combinations**: inside the flagged mask, thickness separates them (10.2 and 12.8 px against 18.0 px
  next, 682_z23 (107,13)), and so does minor axis, but across only 0.7 px (19.8 against 20.5). Outside
  the flagged mask, 682_z29 (449,250) is as thin (12.2 px) and keeps 0.706 after the opening; by eye
  that one should be checked too.
- **For the 3.6 variant** the filter is: reject a kept object whose share left after the r = 5 opening
  is below 0.515, the middle of the gap from 0.348 to 0.681. On the default results it rejects exactly
  the two band objects. The threshold rests on 2 objects; it is placed in the gap, not fitted.
- 682_z23 (363,7) touches the frame edge, so it is already out of every per-cell mean. Rejecting it
  changes only the lacuna count and the graph.
