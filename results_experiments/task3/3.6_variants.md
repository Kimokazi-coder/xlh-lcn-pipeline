# 3.6 Network-level effect of the lacuna variants

Pre-validation, px. Each variant changes the lacunae only; the network stage then runs as in the
pipeline (canal mask, preprocessed channel and hysteresis mask from the default run, which do not
depend on the lacunae; lacuna removal, skeleton, bridging, graph, roots and rings recomputed).
Changed shapes (opening, hole fill) go through the pipeline's own filters again.

- crumbs_join_merge (3.1): watershed pieces under 400 px^2 may join the re-merge test.
- widest_path_merge (3.2): the re-merge uses the widest-path saddle instead of the straight line.
- band_filter (3.3): also reject objects keeping less than 0.515 of their area after an
  r = 5 opening.
- open_r2, open_r3, open_r4 (3.4): each kept lacuna replaced by the largest piece of its opening.
- fill_holes_200 (3.5): enclosed holes up to 200 px^2 filled.

- **crumbs_join_merge** changes exactly the components found in 3.1, plus two that 3.1 did not list
  because no kept lacuna was in them:
  - 543_3 (877,545): the missing middle band is filled; the cell is whole and the thread traced
    through it disappears (image roots per cell -1.6%).
  - 542_z18 (936,998) gains its 235 px^2 crumb (roots per cell +6.4%, ring 30 +7.3% for the image,
    because one cell's values change in a field of 11).
  - 542_z06 (106,67), the leaked outline, grows further (its centroid moves to (108,56)).
  - New objects: 682_z29 (862,728), two pieces of 346 and 229 px^2 that now merge into one object
    above 400 px^2 (the dim object of the archived decision D8); and 682_z23 (74,8) at the top edge.
  - So the variant fixes the gap but also admits objects that were only crumbs. Crops in 3.7.
- **widest_path_merge** changes two places: 682_z29 (100,835) becomes one cell (13 to 12 lacunae; its
  roots and ring 30 per cell rise 10% because one cell now has the length of two), and the 542_z06
  band object disappears (merged with the band piece above it, then rejected), as in 3.2.
- **band_filter** rejects exactly the two band objects (table above); in 542_z06 it gives the same result
  as the widest-path merge. Roots and ring 30 per cell rise 4.5% and 4.3% in 542_z06 because the band
  object had 4 roots and little ring length, and its neighbour (635,143) gains the ring pixels it held.
  682_z23 (363,7) touches the frame, so only its count changes.
- **Opening** touches almost every cell. At r = 3, roots per cell falls up to 8.1% (543_3) and ring 30
  by 1.3 to 12.0% (542_z06 most), because the smaller bodies have shorter boundaries and their rings
  start further in. Field density rises slightly (up to +0.67%) because less area is buffered away
  around the lacunae. 3 lacunae fall below 400 px^2 at r = 3 and 5 at r = 4.
- **fill_holes_200** changes 542_z06 (783,581) only, by 146 px^2, with no effect on any network measure
  except +0.01% in field density.
- The per-field ordering of the headline measures is the same under every variant here. The opening
  is the only variant that moves every image, and it is the only one whose effect is mostly a size
  effect (4.1).
