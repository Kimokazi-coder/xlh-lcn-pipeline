# 3.1 Crumb loss audit

Pre-validation, px. For every kept lacuna: its pre-watershed component (the lacuna mask after
the cut, hole fill and r=1 opening) against what the pipeline keeps of it. missing % is the
share of the component in no kept lacuna. It splits into dropped pieces (watershed pieces
removed by a filter, almost always area < 400 px^2) and pixels the watershed never labels (it
floods with 4-connectivity, while components are 8-connected, so pixels joined only
diagonally stay 0). adjacent is the part of the dropped pieces that shares its longest boundary
with this lacuna, as a share of the lacuna's own area. When a component holds more than one
kept lacuna, its missing % is shared by all of them.

Kept lacunae: 98. Whose component misses more than 3%: 4. With any adjacent
dropped piece: 4. With unlabelled pixels: 1.

## Reading

- **Crumb loss is rare and concentrated in odd objects.** 93 of 98 kept lacunae keep their whole
  pre-watershed component. One more (542_z06 at (909,17)) misses 23 px^2 (1.6%) that the watershed never
  labels. The 4 lacunae missing more than 3% are:
  - 542_z06 (555,149), the band object: its component is a longer stretch of the band, and the kept
    piece holds only 36% of it.
  - 542_z06 (106,67), the leaked outline (round 3 finding 2): 42% of its component is dropped.
  - 542_z18 (938,990): a 235 px^2 piece beside a 508 px^2 lacuna, 46% of the lacuna's own area.
  - 543_3 (877,545): a 124 px^2 piece, 9.2% of the lacuna's area. This is the named saddle point
    543_3 (875,545) of task 3.2.
- Every dropped piece in these components was removed for area (< 400 px^2), never for shape.
- **For the 3.6 variant** (pieces under 400 px^2 may join the re-merge test), these 4 components are the
  only places where it can change anything. Whether a crumb joins depends on its saddle ratio, so the
  variant can grow the two odd objects as well as the two ordinary lacunae.
