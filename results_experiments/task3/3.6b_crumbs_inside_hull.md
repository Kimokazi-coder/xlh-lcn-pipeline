# 3.6b A narrower crumb rule: crumbs inside the convex hull

Pre-validation, px. Added after 3.6 and 3.7, because the 3.6 crumb variant fixed the 543_3 gap but
also admitted new objects. Rule: after the pipeline's re-merge and filters, a dropped watershed
piece joins a kept lacuna only if it touches that one kept lacuna and at least
0.5 of it lies inside the lacuna's convex hull.

## Reading

- The narrow rule joins exactly one crumb in the 8 images, the 124 px^2 middle piece of 543_3 (877,545).
  That cell becomes whole (1344 to 1468 px^2), the thread traced through its gap disappears, and its
  roots fall from 6 to 5 and ring 30 from 277 to 269 px. Image means: roots per cell -1.6%, ring 30
  -0.3% in 543_3; nothing changes in the other 7 images (the remaining differences of about 1e-5 % in
  density are rounding against the cached 8-decimal value).
- Unlike the 3.6 crumb variant it admits no new object and does not grow the band object or the leaked
  outline, because those crumbs lie outside their lacuna's hull (shares 0.000 to 0.009).
- The threshold sits in a gap from 0.009 to 1.000, but the gap is defined by 4 pieces only.
