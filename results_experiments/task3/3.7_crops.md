## Reading (what the crops show)

- **543-2 (40,300) and (265,95), 543_3 (920,200)**: the default outlines include a tail along a thread
  root or a broad side lobe. The opening at r = 2 to 4 removes little of either, because the tail and
  the lobe are wider than the disk; it trims only a few pixels at the tips, and the skeleton grows a
  few pixels into the freed space (yellow). The opening does not fix these cases.
- **542_z06 (105,65)**: the leaked tail is removed by the r = 3 opening. The crumb variant makes the
  outline larger instead.
- **542_z06 (783,581)**: the hole is filled; nothing else changes.
- **Serrated 682 cells** (682_z08 (210,434), 682_z23 (472,61), 682_z29 (182,716)): the serration is the
  bases of the canaliculi included in the outline as small bumps. The opening removes the bumps and
  each root's skeleton grows 2 to 4 px into the freed space, which changes ring lengths slightly and
  roots rarely.
- **542_z06 band pair (570,100)**: with the band object gone (widest path or band filter), the
  hysteresis mask along the band wall is no longer cut by it, and the skeleton traces a line along the
  wall in its place (yellow). Removing the object therefore adds about its length of band-wall
  skeleton; it does not leave a clean band.
- **682_z29 (100,835)**: under the widest path the two pieces become one outline. By eye it reads as
  one elongated cell with a dimmer, narrower left part, but this is Karim's call.
- **543_3 (877,545)**: the crumb variant makes the cell whole and removes the skeleton traced across
  the gap (red). The r = 2 opening instead keeps only the upper lobe, and the skeleton then runs
  through the lower half of the body (yellow): the opening makes this case worse.
- **542_z18 (937,993)**: the crumb joins the lacuna at its lower end.
- **682_z29 (862,728)**: the crumb variant creates a lacuna on a dim, hazy elongated body that the
  default traces as a thread. Whether this is a lacuna partly out of the focal plane is the open D4
  question.
- **682_z23 (74,8)**: a new object at the top edge, flagged on_border, so it changes the count only.
