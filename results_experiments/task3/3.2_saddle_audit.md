# 3.2 Saddle audit

component, which is the set of pairs the pipeline's re-merge tests. ratio_straight is the
pipeline's measure: the lowest distance-transform value on the straight line between the two
peaks, over the smaller peak. ratio_widest uses the widest path inside the mask instead: the
largest h such that the two peaks stay 8-connected through pixels with distance >= h, over the
smaller peak. The cut 0.35 is not retuned.

## Reading

- **The re-merge test is rare.** Over the 8 images only 15 pairs of substantial pieces (each at least
  400 px^2) share a component, in 13 components. 11 of them have a straight-line ratio of 0.556 or
  more and merge either way.
- **The widest path is never lower than the straight line here**, as expected: the straight line is
  one of the paths. It differs where the straight line leaves the mask (ratio 0.000) or crosses a
  thin part.
- **3 pairs flip their own test at 0.35, and 2 final states change:**
  - 542_z06 (584,53) with (555,149): straight 0.000, widest 0.566. These are two stretches of the
    vertical band; (555,149) is the band object of 3.3. Under the widest path they merge, and the merged
    object fails a shape filter, so 542_z06 goes from 16 to 15 kept objects and the band object
    disappears.
  - 682_z29 (76,831) with (125,842): straight 0.000, widest 0.559. This is the split the 0.35 cut was
    calibrated to keep ("the one true two-lobe separation, ratio 0.000"). The straight line between
    the two peaks leaves the mask, but a path of width 0.56 of the smaller peak joins them. Under the
    widest path they become one lacuna and 682_z29 goes from 13 to 12. By eye (overlay crop) the two
    pieces form one elongated outline with a neck near the left; whether that is one cell or two is a
    by-eye call for Karim. Crops in 3.7.
  - 542_z06 (230,275) with (220,350): straight 0.242, widest 0.606. Already merged through the chain
    via (230,311), so nothing changes. This is the long curved body of 2.1, rejected later on solidity.
- **543_3 near (875,545)**: one pair, (873,528) with (881,566), straight 0.910, widest 0.979. Merged
  either way. The 124 px^2 crumb of 3.1 belongs to this lacuna.
- **682_z08 near (435,545) and (555,520)**: no tested pair. These are two separate kept lacunae
  (862 and 1521 px^2), each a single component at the cut, about 120 px apart. The brightest path
  between them dips to 0.314, half of t_hi (0.625), so they stay apart at any nearby cut and the
  re-merge never sees them. Whether they are one cell partly out of the focal plane is not something
  the saddle rule can decide.
- **542_z06 splits**: 6 tested pairs; the 3 that the 0.35 calibration was built on (ratio at least
  0.364) merge under both measures.
- The distribution has no values between 0.364 and 0.556 under the straight line, and none below 0.559
  under the widest path, so neither measure puts a pair close to 0.35 except (230,311)-(220,350)
  at 0.364.
