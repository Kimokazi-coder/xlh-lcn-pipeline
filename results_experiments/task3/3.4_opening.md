## Reading

- **Most outlines are smooth at these scales.** At r = 3 the median lacuna loses 1.2% of its area and
  3.0% of its perimeter; 5 of 98 lose more than 10%. No lacuna disappears at any radius; 2 (r = 3) and
  4 (r = 4) fall below 400 px^2 after the opening, all among the smallest objects.
- **The biggest losses are specific objects, not a general tail problem:**
  - 543_3 (877,545) loses 47% already at r = 2. This is not a tail. Its kept mask is two lobes with a
    6-row band missing between them: the watershed made three pieces (722, 124 and 622 px^2), the
    re-merge joined the two large ones, and the 124 px^2 middle piece, too small to enter the re-merge
    test, was dropped by the area filter (it is the crumb of 3.1). The opening then separates the
    lobes and keeps one. The network skeleton has 8 px inside that missing band, so a thread is traced
    across the middle of this cell. The 3.1 variant (crumbs may join the re-merge) is the fix this case
    needs; 3.6 tests it.
  - 542_z06 (106,67), the leaked outline: 22% at r = 2, 25% at r = 3, the tail into the canalicular loop.
  - 682_z23 (363,7) and 542_z06 (555,149), the band objects: 12% and 5% at r = 3, 25% and 19% at r = 4.
  - 542_z06 (724,817) 12% and 682_z29 (449,250) 11% at r = 3: thin objects (minor axis 18.8 and 12.3 px).
- **Serrated edges**: the perimeter falls more than the area. 682_z08 (210,434) loses 21% of its
  perimeter for 6% of its area at r = 3; 542_z06 (870,202) 26% for 5.5%; 542_z06 has the largest median
  perimeter change of any image (-10.7%). Aspect ratios barely move (median -0.011 at r = 3).
- **542_z06 (230,300)** (the rejected curved body of 2.1) is not in this table because it is not kept.
