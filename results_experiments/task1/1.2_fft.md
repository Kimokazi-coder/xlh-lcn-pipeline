# 1.2 FFT peaks and banding

Pre-validation, px. 2D power spectrum of the raw red channel (8-bit grey levels) and of the
preprocessed channel, Hann window, mean removed. Strength is power over the median power in a
21 x 21 bin window around the frequency (the local spectrum). Periods above 64 px are left out
(the cells and canals themselves). wave_angle_deg is the direction the intensity varies along
(0 = along x, so the stripes are vertical; 90 = along y, horizontal stripes).

Noise reference: where there is no periodic signal the periodogram is close to exponential, so
power over local median behaves like Exp/ln 2 and the largest of N tested bins is expected near
log2(N). noise_expected_max gives that value (about 18 for the ~260,000 bins of one half plane).
A peak near it is what noise alone produces.

## Reading

- **No dominant periodic peak at lattice scale.** Between periods of 2 and 64 px, the strongest bins
  stand 17 to 30 times above their local spectrum, against about 18 expected from noise alone. The
  maps in `1.2_per_image/` show scattered single bins, no lines, no repeated grid. A lattice made by
  the instrument at a fixed spacing of 8 px or more would give a sharp peak far above that.
- **Two pixel-scale artefacts in the raw red channel of all 8 images.**
  - Alternating columns (period 2 px along x, the Nyquist bin u = 512): 290 to 870 times the local
    spectrum. After removing a 32 px moving mean, even and odd columns differ by 0.10 to 0.11 grey
    levels of 255.
  - Period 4.00 px along x (u = 256): 10 to 30 times the local spectrum, strongest in 542_z06,
    542_z18, 682_z08, 682_z23 and 682_z29. Peak-to-peak 0.14 to 0.16 grey levels.
  - Nothing comparable along y (the y bins are at noise level; the folded y amplitude is 0.02 to
    0.04 grey levels). The artefact runs along x only, as from the pixel clock or the digitizer
    rather than from line scanning.
  - The preprocessing (Gaussian sigma 0.8 and top-hat) cuts these to 1 to 50 times the local
    spectrum and 0.03 to 0.07 grey-level equivalents. Thread contrast is tens of grey levels.
- **Banding in the row and column mean profiles.** The column profiles show the same period-4 comb
  (14 to 39 times their local spectrum, null about 9). The row profiles show nothing consistent: the
  strongest periods vary from image to image (2 to 8 px) at 7 to 27 times, and they do not repeat
  across images. No slow banding across rows or columns stands out.
- **Low-frequency peaks near the x axis in 542_z06** (periods 35 to 64 px, varying along x, up to 29
  times): vertical structures repeating across the field, the vertical canal and the vertical rows
  of lacunae in that image. Tissue, not an instrument period.

So the periodic content of the raw data is real but sub-grey-level and 2 to 4 px in period. It
cannot by itself draw a lattice of runs 8 px and longer. Task 1.3 tests the lattice directly.
