# 1.4 Notch filter variant

Gaussian notches (sigma 1 bin) at period 2 px and period 4 px along x, the two peaks of 1.2 that
stand above noise in every image. notch_network_only keeps the default lacunae and runs the
network stage (canal mask, preprocessing, threshold, bridging, graph) on the filtered channel.
notch_both_stages runs the lacuna stage on it as well. bin_x2_after and bin_x4_after are the
two bins' power over their local spectrum after the notch (before: 290 to 870 and 10 to 30).

## Reading

- **The notch removes both artefacts** (after it, the two bins stand 0.0 to 1.4 times their local
  spectrum) by changing no pixel by more than 0.17 grey levels of 255.
- **It changes almost nothing downstream. This is a null result.**
  - Skeleton: 163 to 509 pixels move per image (0.4 to 1.1% of the skeleton), with the net skeleton
    length changing by at most 21 px.
  - Field density: -0.03% to +0.05%.
  - Roots per cell: unchanged in 6 of 8 images; 543_z13 +1.4%; 542_z06 -0.9% when the lacuna stage
    is also run on the filtered channel.
  - Ring 30 px per cell: -0.06% to +0.32%.
  - Lacuna counts: unchanged in every image.
  - Bridges: 1 or 2 more or fewer per image (-12.5% to +11.8% of small counts), the noisiest output.
- **The 682_z08 lattice stays** (axis-run share in the box 0.333 before, 0.330 after), which confirms
  that it does not come from these artefacts.
- So the pixel-scale artefacts do not need correcting for the current measures. The notch can stay an
  experiment.
