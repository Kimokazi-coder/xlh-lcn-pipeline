red_empty_levels_inside_range counts 8-bit levels between the red minimum and maximum that
no pixel uses. A value above 0 means the histogram is combed, the sign of a contrast stretch
applied after the data were already 8-bit.
## Reading

- All 8 files are 8-bit RGB renderings, 1024 x 1024, one page. None carries a Software tag or any
  microscope metadata (no OME, LSM or vendor tags). They are exports, not raw confocal files, so the
  original bit depth, pixel size, zoom and scan settings cannot be read from them.
- 7 files were last written by ImageJ 1.54p (ImageDescription "ImageJ=1.54p"), uncompressed, with no
  resolution tags.
- 542_z06 differs: RGBA with a constant alpha of 255, LZW compression with a horizontal predictor, and
  300 x 300 per inch, a generic export value, not a calibration. It was written by a different program.
- The red histograms are not combed (0 unused levels between the minimum and maximum), so no contrast
  stretch after 8-bit conversion is visible. 0.10 to 0.96% of red pixels are saturated at 255.
- Green is essentially empty (maximum 13 to 18 of 255). Blue has a mean of 12.6 to 27.7 (see task 5.2).
