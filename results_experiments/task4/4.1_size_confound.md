## Reading

- **The observation holds.** Across the 8 images, roots per cell rises with median lacuna area
  (Spearman rho = 0.838, p = 0.009) and so does ring 30 px per cell (rho = 0.881, p = 0.004), close to
  the 0.85 to 0.9 estimated by hand. Field density does not (rho = -0.52, p = 0.18).
- **It holds per cell too, and inside every image.** Pooled over the 86 interior lacunae: roots against
  area rho = 0.69, ring 30 against area rho = 0.68. Within each image rho is positive in 8 of 8 images
  for both (median 0.50 for roots, 0.61 for ring 30). So it is not only that some images have bigger
  cells; bigger cells score higher in the same field.
- **The mechanism in the measures is geometric.** Ring area at 30 px tracks lacuna area at rho = 0.94
  (perimeter at 0.93): a bigger body has a longer boundary and a larger 30 px ring, so it collects more
  attachment points and more skeleton at the same network density.
- **Both normalised measures remove the size dependence.**
  - Roots per 100 px of perimeter: pooled rho = -0.13 (p = 0.23), within-image median -0.04 (3 of 8
    positive), between images -0.24 (p = 0.57).
  - Ring density L_30 / A_30: pooled rho = 0.03 (p = 0.78), within-image median 0.13 (4 of 8 positive),
    between images -0.02 (p = 0.96). At 60 px: -0.06, 0.14, -0.31.
  - They also spread less across images: coefficient of variation 0.119 for roots per 100 px against
    0.184 for roots, and 0.085 for ring density 30 against 0.147 for ring 30.
- **The frame is not a factor for interior lacunae.** 85 of 86 have an in-frame fraction of at least
  0.9 at 30 px (minimum 0.884), and 81 at 60 px. Restricting to them changes no conclusion.
- **Cautions.**
  - The 8 images are probably sections of 3 fields (6.1), so the same cells appear more than once.
    The pooled n = 86 overstates the independent cells and the between-image n = 8 is closer to 3.
    The p-values are therefore optimistic.
  - That the size dependence is removed does not say which form is right. Bigger lacunae may truly carry
    more canaliculi (a biological size effect), in which case roots per cell is the honest count and the
    normalised forms answer a different question (canaliculi per unit of surface, network per unit of
    surrounding area). Which one the thesis needs is under Decisions needed.
  - Roots per 100 px perimeter uses the pixel perimeter of the segmented outline, so a serrated outline
    (3.4) inflates the perimeter and lowers the value.
