# AI lacuna and POL judges on: quantification (WT)

Pre-validation, not validated: no verdict of either judge has been checked against a manual count. Made on 2026-11-09 with the method at tag `method-freeze-v2` and the judge rerun of that day (docs/METHODS.md section 29; report `results/archive/judge/REPORT_JUDGE.md`). The deterministic results beside this folder are not changed by it.

Every measured number of WT from the lacunae accepted by the AI lacuna judge: shown every candidate region of the lacuna stage, numbered, it said per region yes, no or unsure with a reason code, under the working POL definition (enlarged lacunae and clusters kept; only fragments, band, tissue and saturated border pieces rejected). It never draws a boundary, so every lacuna has the lacuna stage's own outline. A region mostly in the excluded region is not offered to it. A region it added that the filters had rejected is the class `judged`, with no area or shape measure. Then the AI POL judge (2026-11-09, docs/METHODS.md section 30) looked at every candidate POL among those lacunae (above 76.6 um2, flagged cluster, or closer than 3 um to another) in a crop and said whether it is an enlarged lacuna, a cluster, pieces of one body (merged as the union of their own pixels) or no lacuna (removed); its cluster decisions set the cluster flag. Its log is in `3_pols/ai_judge/ai_log/`. The POL section of this set is `3_pols/ai_judge/`.

Made from the logs, offline: `LCN_HOLDOUT_MODE=FINAL python src/diagnostics.py ai-lacuna-run --with-pol-judge`. The calls: `ai-lacuna-judge --live` and `ai-pol-judge --live`. Held-out images are in the set, judged as part of the method, but their judge outputs were not opened, reviewed or summarized (docs/HOLDOUT.md). The comparison with the judge off, on the development images, is in `results/archive/judge/JUDGE_ON_VS_OFF.md` and the sheet "Judge on vs off" of `results/SUMMARY.xlsx`.
