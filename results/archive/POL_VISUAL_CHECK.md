other groups the lacuna stage, shaped on WT, measures band and soft tissue
pieces and many small fragments as lacunae, splits lacunae, and keeps a few
large irregular shapes that may be merged lacunae or tissue. Small fragments
pull a group's median area down and band and tissue pieces push its upper
tail up, so the group statistics of lacunar size carry these errors. None of
this was fixed here; it is the detection the pictures show.
