THE RULES, IN WORDS

**1. The POL is the enlarged, dye-filled lacunar space as outlined.** No halo
around it is part of the decision.

**2. One body, one lacuna.** If one dye-filled space is cut into several
outlines with no bone between the pieces, it is a split: merge the pieces.

**3. Several bodies in one outline are a cluster,** kept and counted as a
cluster; several bodies in separate outlines that only lie close are separate
lacunae.

**4. Reject only what is not a lacuna:** a band piece, a tissue piece, or a
saturated border piece. Never reject for size or irregular shape.
